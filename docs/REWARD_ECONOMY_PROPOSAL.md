# TON ARENA — Reward Economy Proposal

**Status:** Proposal for research and validation; not approved for launch  
**Last updated:** 2026-11-06

## Product idea

Explore a future "play, learn, compete, earn" experience in which eligible players can receive TON rewards for verified gameplay, learning activities, and community competitions.

This is a reward program, not device mining. TON is a proof-of-stake blockchain; the app must never imply that a phone mines TON by running the game. Rewards, if introduced, are funded from an explicitly budgeted reward reserve.

## Non-negotiable principles

1. **No misleading mining claim.** Describe the product as skill-based rewards, not TON mining or passive income.
2. **Transparent reward rules.** Explain eligibility, calculation, caps, timing, withdrawal minimums, fees, exclusions, and program changes before a player participates.
3. **No hidden split.** Do not conceal a 50/50 allocation or any other material distribution. Publish the reward formula and the platform's funding/operating model in understandable terms.
4. **No user-funded prize pool.** Do not require an entry fee, wager, deposit, or purchase to qualify for a chance-based prize. Keep the MVP free.
5. **Skill and learning first.** Reward verified quality and effort, not raw screen time, device uptime, ad clicks, referrals alone, or compulsive engagement.
6. **Budget before promises.** Never promise a fixed TON amount until a funded reserve, payout costs, fraud losses, and operating runway have been modelled.
7. **Abuse resistance.** Design server-authoritative scoring, rate limits, device/account abuse signals, Sybil resistance, payout holds, and an appeal path before enabling transfers.
8. **Regional compliance.** Review applicable gaming, consumer-protection, advertising, tax, sanctions, AML/KYC, privacy, and crypto-asset rules for each launch market. Obtain qualified legal advice; do not claim the program is universally legal or religiously permissible.
9. **Platform compliance.** Re-check Telegram Mini App, Telegram Stars, TON Connect, and wallet/payment requirements at implementation time.
10. **Healthy engagement.** No manipulative streak pressure, artificial scarcity, or messaging that encourages excessive play.

## Candidate reward design (not yet approved)

- Players earn non-transferable in-app points/XP for verified skill-game results and learning milestones.
- A separate seasonal reward pool may distribute TON to eligible participants under published rules.
- Reward calculations can combine skill score, verified participation, learning completion, and fair-play status, with per-account and per-season caps.
- A public season page should show the total funded pool, distribution formula, claim window, and remaining allocation.
- Rewards should be claimable only after fraud checks; no automatic payout promise should be shown before funds and eligibility are confirmed.
- The company may reserve funds for platform operations, taxes, compliance, infrastructure, and future development, but any material allocation affecting user rewards must be disclosed clearly.

## Why not promise 50% immediately?

A fixed 50/50 split is not yet grounded in revenue, user economics, payout fees, fraud rates, or legal review. A percentage of an undefined "volume" is ambiguous. First model:
- what generates revenue or funds the pool;
- whether TON is bought, donated, sponsored, or otherwise lawfully sourced;
- what amount is reserved for players and when it is funded;
- network and payout costs;
- fraud and chargeback exposure;
- operating, tax, and compliance costs;
- how the company remains solvent during low-revenue periods.

Only then choose a sustainable allocation and explain it to users.

## Launch gates

Do not enable TON payouts until all are true:
- [ ] Legal and platform review completed for target launch countries.
- [ ] Reward source and reserve are funded and independently reconcilable.
- [ ] Public reward terms and privacy disclosures are ready.
- [ ] Anti-cheat, multi-account, bot, and payout-abuse controls are tested.
- [ ] Payout wallet custody, signing, limits, monitoring, and recovery are designed.
- [ ] Tax/accounting treatment and customer support/appeals are defined.
- [ ] A small closed pilot demonstrates sustainable unit economics.

## MVP decision

The current BRAIN SPRINT MVP remains free, with XP, levels, missions, and friendly leaderboards only. No TON payout, token issuance, wallet connection, mining, wager, or entry fee is enabled in the MVP.

## Open research questions

- Which initial countries can be supported compliantly?
- What is the actual source of reward funding?
- Should rewards be seasonal, achievement-based, sponsored, or a combination?
- What minimum payout and cadence keep fees reasonable without disadvantaging small players?
- What disclosures and controls are required for minors and other protected users?
